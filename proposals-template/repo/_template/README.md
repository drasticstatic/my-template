## <img src="../assets/icons/compass.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> TL;DR

> [!TIP]
> **Five lines, if that's all you read today.**
>
> 1. <img src="../assets/icons/sparkles.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **What:** the change, in one sentence.
> 2. <img src="../assets/icons/heart.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Why it matters:** who it helps, and how.
> 3. <img src="../assets/icons/circle-check.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Already true:** what's done or ready, with a link to see it.
> 4. <img src="../assets/icons/users.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Asked of you:** the decision or answer needed, and from whom.
> 5. <img src="../assets/icons/wrench.svg" width="16" height="16" alt=""> **Until then:** what keeps running as it is. Nothing changes by itself.

# Your Organization's Proposals

*Where ideas become plans, and plans go to the people they affect.*

</div>

## <img src="assets/icons/compass.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> TL;DR

Each proposal is one folder with one `README.md`, written so anyone can follow it. A TL;DR comes first,
the technical detail sits in fold-out sections, and every idea gets an icon beside its words. Each
proposal is also rendered automatically as a **PDF** in [`pdf/`](pdf/), easy to send by email or share
in the community.

## <img src="assets/icons/route.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> How a proposal is shaped

1. **TL;DR**: five lines or fewer.
2. **What's offered**, and **why**.
3. **Itinerary**: the stops, each marked done as it happens.
4. **Questions**: what we need from the people it affects.
5. **Decisions log**: what was chosen, when, and by whom.
6. **Details**: technical material in fold-out sections.

## <img src="assets/icons/users.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> How we decide

A proposal is shared with the people it affects, by email, a PDF or a link. They decide, and the outcome
is written into its decisions log with the date and who chose. Discussion can also happen in this repo's
**Issues**, one per proposal. A proposal never changes anything by itself.
